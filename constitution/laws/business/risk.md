# Article VI: Risk Management Laws

## Section 6.1: Risk Assessment Law

**Law ID:** `BUS-6.1`

Risks MUST be systematically assessed.

### Risk Assessment Frequency

- Annual comprehensive assessment
- Project-specific assessments for new initiatives
- Event-triggered assessments after incidents

### Risk Assessment Template

```markdown
## Risk: [ID] - [Name]

**Category:** [ ] Strategic [ ] Operational [ ] Financial [ ] Compliance [ ] Reputational

**Description:** [What could happen]

**Likelihood:** [ ] Rare [ ] Unlikely [ ] Possible [ ] Likely [ ] Almost Certain
**Impact:** [ ] Insignificant [ ] Minor [ ] Moderate [ ] Major [ ] Severe

**Risk Score:** [Likelihood × Impact]

**Current Controls:**
- [Control 1]
- [Control 2]

**Residual Risk:** [After controls]

**Treatment:**
[ ] Accept [ ] Mitigate [ ] Transfer [ ] Avoid

**Mitigation Plan:**
- [Action 1] - Owner: [Name] - Due: [Date]

**Risk Owner:** [Name]
**Review Date:** [Next review]
```

## Section 6.2: Risk Register Law

**Law ID:** `BUS-6.2`

All significant risks MUST be tracked.

### Risk Register Fields

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| ID | Unique identifier |
| Name | Short description |
| Category | Risk type |
| Likelihood | Probability of occurrence |
| Impact | Consequence severity |
| Score | Calculated risk level |
| Owner | Accountable person |
| Status | Open, mitigating, closed |
| Review Date | Next review |

---

## Section 6.3: Risk Appetite Law

**Law ID:** `BUS-6.3`

Risk appetite MUST be defined and communicated.

### Risk Appetite Statement

| Category | Appetite | Boundary |
|----------|----------|----------|
| Compliance | Zero tolerance | No knowing violations |
| Security | Low | Protect customer data absolutely |
| Operational | Moderate | Accept some downtime for innovation |
| Financial | Moderate | Invest for growth within limits |
| Reputational | Low | Protect brand and trust |

---

## Section 6.4: Risk Reporting Law

**Law ID:** `BUS-6.4`

Risks MUST be reported to appropriate levels.

| Risk Level | Report To | Frequency |
|------------|-----------|-----------|
| Critical | CEO, Board | Immediately |
| High | Executive team | Weekly |
| Medium | Department heads | Monthly |
| Low | Risk owner | Quarterly |

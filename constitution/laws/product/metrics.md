# Article VI: Product-Market Fit Laws

## Section 6.1: PMF Definition Law

**Law ID:** `PRD-6.1`

Product-Market Fit SHALL be measured, not assumed.

### PMF Indicators

| Signal | Measure | PMF Threshold |
|--------|---------|---------------|
| **Retention** | Week 4 / Week 8 cohort | >40% returning |
| **NPS** | Net Promoter Score | >40 |
| **Sean Ellis Test** | "Very disappointed" if gone | >40% |
| **Organic Growth** | Word of mouth referrals | >25% of acquisition |
| **Usage Frequency** | DAU/MAU ratio | >25% |

## Section 6.2: Retention Over Acquisition Law

## Reduce Churn

**Law ID:** `PRD-6.2` | **Status:** NON-NEGOTIABLE

Retention MUST be prioritized over acquisition.

### Wrong vs Right Priority

```
❌ WRONG PRIORITY
"Let's get more users" (leaky bucket)

✅ CORRECT PRIORITY
"Let's keep users coming back" (then acquire more)
```

### Retention Analysis Required

- Cohort retention curves
- Churn reason analysis
- Resurrection campaigns
- Power user vs. casual patterns

## Section 6.3: Pivot Criteria Law

**Law ID:** `PRD-6.3`

Pivots MUST be evidence-based, not panic-driven.

### Pivot Signals

- Retention below threshold for 3+ months
- Core assumption invalidated
- Market shift makes current approach unviable
- Better opportunity discovered

### Pivot Process

1. Document current state (what's not working)
2. Analyze root cause (why it's not working)
3. Identify pivot options (what else could work)
4. Validate new direction (minimum experiment)
5. Commit and communicate (clear decision)

### Pivot Types

| Type | What Changes | What Stays |
|------|--------------|------------|
| **Zoom-in** | Feature becomes product | Customer, problem |
| **Zoom-out** | Product becomes feature | Customer |
| **Customer** | New customer segment | Problem, solution |
| **Problem** | Different problem | Customer |
| **Channel** | Distribution method | Product |
| **Technology** | Technical approach | Customer, problem |

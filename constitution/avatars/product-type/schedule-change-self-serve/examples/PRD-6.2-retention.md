# PRD-6.2: Retention & Engagement

Define retention metrics at launch. Feature is retained only if 30-day active usage ≥ target threshold.

## Application to Schedule Change Self Serve

This product domain applies PRD-6.2 by requiring all feature work to demonstrate compliance
with the retention & engagement principle before advancing through the stage gate.

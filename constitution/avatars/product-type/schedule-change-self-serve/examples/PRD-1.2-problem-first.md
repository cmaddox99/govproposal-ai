# PRD-1.2: Problem-First Validation

Define the problem statement and evidence before any solution framing. Link to customer research, NPS drivers, or operational data.

## Application to Schedule Change Self Serve

This product domain applies PRD-1.2 by requiring all feature work to demonstrate compliance
with the problem-first validation principle before advancing through the stage gate.

See [PRD Article I](../../../../laws/product/foundations.md) and [PRD Article II](../../../../laws/product/discovery.md) for full law text.

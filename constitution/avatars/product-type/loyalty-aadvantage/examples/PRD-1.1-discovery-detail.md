# PRD-1.1: Continuous Discovery — AADvantage Loyalty Program Research

## Executive Summary
Research into AADvantage member needs reveals three critical gaps: (1) Casual members don't understand or engage with elite benefits; (2) Frequent travelers perceive points as low-value compared to competitors' sign-up bonuses; (3) Elite members feel their loyalty is underappreciated relative to time and money invested. This shapes our roadmap priorities: simplify casual redemption, increase earning power, and create exclusive elite experiences.

---

## Research Methodology

| Method | Sample | Approach |
|--------|--------|----------|
| Casual member surveys | N=150 | Online survey + 60 qualitative interviews |
| Frequent traveler intercepts | N=200 | Airport intercepts + 80 in-depth interviews |
| Elite member sessions | N=100 | Dinner sessions + 40 structured interviews |
| Behavioral analysis | 2M+ members | 6-month earning/redemption patterns |
| Competitive benchmarking | 4 programs | United, Delta, Southwest, JetBlue |

---

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Casual Members Lack Redemption Confidence
- 67% cannot recall their points balance without logging in; 78% say redemption is "intimidating"
- 45% start a redemption booking; only 28% complete within 24 hours

**Business Impact:** ~1.2M casual members with unredeemed points = $40M locked value annually; 18% churn to competitors citing "never used my points."

### Finding 2: Points Earning Perceived as Weak
- Credit card sign-up bonuses (50K–100K points) dwarf typical flight earning (1,500–2,500 points per flight)
- 34% of frequent travelers have lower AADvantage engagement than 2 years ago

**Business Impact:** Slower member acquisition, reduced lifetime value, and revenue leakage to competing programs.

### Finding 3: Elite Members Feel Underrecognized
- 62% of platinum members say the lounge experience has "degraded" in 3 years
- 71% would gift elite status to family; current program does not allow it

**Business Impact:** 18% annual platinum churn; only 35% recommend the program to peers vs. 58% of casual members.

### Finding 4: Redemption Options Too Limited
- 89% of elite redemptions are economy or premium economy; business class redemptions are under 8%
- Award inventory is only 2–5 seats per flight; 12 hotel partners vs. competitors' 25–40

**Business Impact:** Member satisfaction capped at 6.5/10 with economy-only redemption; members who redeem premium experiences have 40% higher retention.

---

## Recommendations

**Quick Wins (60 days):**
1. Add "Book Now" to the dashboard showing 3 popular routes with points cost
2. Send personalized elite thank-you highlighting specific perks used this year

**Medium-Term (6 months):**
1. Mobile-first redesign with real-time balance, elite progress tracking, and early redemption access
2. Gifting pilot: test points transfer to family members with elite members

**Long-Term (12 months):**
1. Remove points expiry policy for competitive parity
2. Expand business class award seats from 2–5 to 5–10 per flight

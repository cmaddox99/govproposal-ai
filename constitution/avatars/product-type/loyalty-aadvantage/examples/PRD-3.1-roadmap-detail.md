# PRD-3.1: Roadmap Planning — AADvantage Loyalty Feature Prioritization

## Executive Summary
The AADvantage 2026 roadmap focuses on three pillars: (1) **Casual Member Activation**—simplify redemption to lift conversion from 45% to 65%; (2) **Frequent Traveler Differentiation**—invest in elite earning transparency; (3) **Elite Member Recognition**—exclusive experiences and gifting to achieve 90%+ retention.

**Prioritization formula:** Member Impact (40%) + Business Value (40%) + Implementation Effort (20%)

---

## Prioritization Criteria Summary

- **Member Impact (40 pts):** How much does this help Sarah redeem, Marcus track elite progress, and Jennifer feel recognized? (0–10 per persona, averaged)
- **Business Value (40 pts):** Revenue potential, churn prevention, member acquisition, competitive differentiation (0–10 each)
- **Implementation Effort (20 pts, inverse):** Lower effort = higher score; 20 = under 5 days, 0 = over 60 days

---

## Tier 1 Features: Q1 2026 (Score 80+)

### Feature 1.1: Mobile App Redesign — Discovery-to-Redemption UX (Score: 88/100)
The current app hasn't been refreshed in 4 years; 62% of users abandon redemption due to 12 undifferentiated options and no elite progress tracking. A mobile-first redesign with personalized dashboard, "Recommended for you" redemption, real-time elite forecast, and savings summary lifts conversion from 45% to 65% and mobile rating to 4.5+. Effort: 90 engineering days.

### Feature 1.2: Award Seat Inventory Expansion (Score: 88/100)
Only 2–3 award seats per flight are available; business class redemptions are under 8% despite 3x higher member satisfaction. Increasing allocation to 5–10 seats/flight via an Operations partnership and reserving 48-hour early access for elite members lifts redemption satisfaction from 6.2/10 to 7.5/10 and elite retention from 82% to 88%. Effort: 60 days (primarily operations negotiation).

### Feature 1.3: Real-Time Elite Progress Tracking (Score: 93/100)
Frequent travelers have no real-time view of their path to elite. A live MQM dashboard with predictive pace model and milestone push notifications increases elite achievement from 25% to 35%, adding ~200K elite members worth an estimated $60M in incremental revenue. Effort: 45 days.

---

## Tier 2 Features: Q2–Q3 2026

### Feature 2.1: Points Gifting & Household Linking
Elite members cannot share points or benefits with family. Enable gifting up to 50K points/year and household account linking; targets elite retention of 90%+ and adds ~50K new members from elite family referrals.

### Feature 2.2: Partner Ecosystem Expansion
Only 12 hotel partners (competitors have 25–40); shopping portal drives under 3% of earning. Adding 15 new partners and activating an Amex shopping portal targets 15% earning mix and +$20–30M annual credit card revenue.

### Feature 2.3: Personalized Elite Concierge
Platinum members wait 15+ minutes on hold with agents unfamiliar with their preferences. Assigning a dedicated in-app concierge with proactive outreach targets elite NPS from 8.2 to 9.2 and retention to 92%.

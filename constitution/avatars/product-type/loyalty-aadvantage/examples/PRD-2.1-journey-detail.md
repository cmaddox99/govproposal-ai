# PRD-2.1: User Journey Mapping — AADvantage Loyalty Program

## Overview
The AADvantage loyalty journey spans enrollment through earning, status achievement, redemption, and retention. This document maps the two primary member flows, identifying friction zones and opportunities.

---

## Journey 1: Casual Member (Sarah) — Enrollment to Annual Redemption

### Phase 1: Enrollment & First Flight
**Flow:** Discovery → Sign-up → Email verification → First flight → Points credited → Welcome email

- **Pain:** 7-field sign-up form causes abandonment; no notification when points post (Sarah checks once, thinks it failed)
- **Opportunity:** One-click SSO enrollment; push + SMS "You earned 2,400 points!" with redemption suggestion; onboarding email with "3 ways to earn faster"

### Phase 2: Passive Earning (Months 1–6)
**Flow:** Multiple flights → Points accumulate → Promotional emails → Balance grows

- **Pain:** Slow accumulation; unclear how many points needed for a free flight; forgotten password on return
- **Opportunity:** Progress message "5 more flights like this and you can redeem to LA!"; biometric login

### Phase 3: Redemption Trigger (Months 7–8)
**Flow:** Trip planning → Remembers points → Tries to redeem → Overwhelmed by options → Abandons

- **Pain:** 12 options per route with varying point values; 23% abandon at seat selection due to analysis paralysis
- **Opportunity:** "Best value" badge on recommended options; pre-selected seats based on previous choices

### Phase 4: Post-Redemption
**Flow:** Flight completion → No follow-up → Low balance → Cycle repeats or drifts to competitor

- **Pain:** No redemption satisfaction request; no engagement until next trip planned
- **Opportunity:** "You saved $450 with points!" summary; "Points expire in 24 months—3 ways to use them"

---

## Journey 2: Frequent Traveler (Marcus) — Elite Path Navigation

### Phase 1: Active Enrollment & Earning (Months 1–3)
**Flow:** Join → Aggressive earning (flights + credit card bonus) → Track elite progress → Strategic planning

- **Pain:** Credit card bonus posts 6–8 weeks later with no confirmation; elite progress bar updates monthly
- **Opportunity:** "Your 50K bonus puts you 150 MQM from Gold!"; live progress with pace forecast

### Phase 2: Elite Status Active (Months 4–12)
**Flow:** Maintain gold → Leverage perks → Monthly check-in on elite progress → Plan path to platinum

- **Pain:** No lounge location map in app; upgrade notification arrives at boarding, not 12 hours earlier
- **Opportunity:** "Lounge available at this airport" pre-check-in; mobile upgrade alert 12 hours before flight

### Phase 3: Redemption Strategy (Months 8–12)
**Flow:** Research sweet spots → Transfer partner points → Book premium cabin → Plan next-year elite maintenance

- **Pain:** Partner transfer rates require 5 clicks to find; business class has only 2–3 award seats available
- **Opportunity:** "1:1 transfer to Hyatt: 25K = 1 night + flight"; "Business class open for August—57K points, book now"

---

## Key Insights

1. **Sarah needs simplification;** Marcus needs transparency; Jennifer needs recognition.
2. Award seat availability has greater impact on satisfaction than earning rate.
3. Post-redemption follow-up is a missed opportunity to celebrate value delivered.
4. Personalization (concierge, gifting) beats marginal point bonuses for elite retention.
5. Exception flows—points expiry, elite status loss, unavailable award seats—need proactive messaging to prevent churn.

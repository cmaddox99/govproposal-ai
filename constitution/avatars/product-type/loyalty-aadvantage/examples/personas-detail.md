# AADvantage Loyalty Program Personas

## Overview
These four personas represent the primary segments of the AADvantage loyalty program, each with distinct needs that shape feature design.

---

## Persona 1: Casual Member (Sarah)

**Role:** Occasional business traveler, leisure flyer | **Age:** 38 | **Annual Flights:** 4–6

### Goals
1. Earn points effortlessly without active account management
2. Redeem points simply for flights home to family
3. Get occasional deals without complexity

### Pain Points
1. Forgets login credentials; points balance unclear between flights
2. Confused by elite tiers and qualification rules—feels excluded
3. Sees competitors offering simpler redemption, considers switching

### Quote
> "I like having the points but honestly I don't think about it much. If redeeming was easier I'd probably do it more often."

### Key Needs
- Simple dashboard with clear balance and expiry reminders
- One-click redemption for popular routes
- Email alerts when points are about to expire

---

## Persona 2: Frequent Traveler (Marcus)

**Role:** Senior consultant, frequent business traveler | **Age:** 45 | **Annual Flights:** 20–30

### Goals
1. Maximize points and redeem for premium cabin upgrades
2. Reach elite status quickly for a better travel experience
3. Consolidate all travel benefits in one program

### Pain Points
1. Points earning feels slow vs. credit card sign-up bonuses
2. Elite qualification rules are complex and keep changing
3. Award seat availability is poor; partner devaluations reduce points value

### Quote
> "I'm flexible on when I fly if it means I can maximize my returns. But I need transparency—if you change the rules, tell me upfront."

### Key Needs
- Real-time elite progress tracking with monthly forecast
- Transparent award availability calendar 30+ days out
- Partner transfer options with clear exchange rates

---

## Persona 3: Elite Member (Jennifer)

**Role:** VP of Operations, platinum elite | **Age:** 52 | **Annual Flights:** 40+

### Goals
1. Maximize travel convenience through elite perks
2. Ensure platinum benefits are worth the time and cost investment
3. Explore premium redemption experiences beyond economy seats

### Pain Points
1. Elite benefits inconsistently applied; lounge experience has degraded
2. Feels undervalued—no options to gift points or status to family
3. Lounge crowding degrades the experience at peak times

### Quote
> "I've been loyal to American for 15 years. I expect the program to recognize that. The little touches matter more than the big discounts."

### Key Needs
- Dedicated concierge support for complex bookings
- Ability to transfer or gift points to family members
- Early access to premium redemption inventory

---

## Persona 4: Program Manager (Diana)

**Role:** Director of Loyalty Product | **Age:** 41 | **Manages:** Product, partnerships, marketing

### Goals
1. Grow member base 15% and increase lifetime value 20%
2. Reduce redemption friction; lift redemption rate from 45% to 65%
3. Prove loyalty program ROI to executive leadership

### Pain Points
1. Data silos prevent real-time member segmentation and personalization
2. Partner integration cycles are slow; competitive programs move faster
3. Executive pressure on short-term metrics vs. long-term member value

### Quote
> "Our members are our most valuable asset. We need to think beyond transactional points—how do we make them feel like insiders?"

### Key Needs
- Real-time analytics: acquisition, activation, and retention dashboards
- Predictive churn modeling for at-risk segments
- Campaign testing platform to rapidly validate hypotheses

---

## Using These Personas

**Product Development:** Choose a primary persona (usually Diana or Marcus) and one secondary. Design solutions that work for both.

**Feature Design:** Ask "Does this help Sarah redeem?" and "Does this help Diana measure ROI?"

**Success Metrics:** Sarah = redemption rate. Marcus = elite retention. Jennifer = NPS. Diana = member LTV.

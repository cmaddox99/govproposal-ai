# PRD-5.1: AADvantage Loyalty — Metrics & KPI Reference

All metrics measured monthly; quarterly executive reviews.

## Tier 1: Member Satisfaction & Engagement

| Metric | Definition | Current | 2026 Q4 Target |
|--------|------------|---------|----------------|
| **NPS** | % Promoters − % Detractors (0-10 scale) | 6.2 | 7.5 (parity with competitors) |
| **Monthly Active Members** | Unique earners/redeemers in calendar month | 2.1M (55% activation) | 3.0M (79% activation) |
| **Redemption Rate** | % active members who redeemed in month | 45% overall | 65% overall |
| **Redemption Satisfaction** | Post-redemption survey score (1-10) | 6.5 | 8.0 |
| **Elite Advancement Rate** | % frequent travelers achieving Gold/Platinum annually | 25% | 40% |
| **Member LTV (blended)** | Net profit per member over 10-year horizon | $650 | $750 |

**Key drivers for Tier 1:** Mobile app experience, award seat availability, elite recognition programs, redemption simplicity.

## Tier 2: Program Health & Operations

| Metric | Definition | Current | 2026 Q4 Target |
|--------|------------|---------|----------------|
| **Award Seat Inventory** | Seats available per flight / % searches with no availability | 2.1 seats / 45% no-availability | 10.0 seats / 10% no-availability |
| **Points Earning Rate** | Average points per dollar of member revenue (all channels) | 1.2 pts/$ | 1.5–1.6 pts/$ |
| **Points Liability Expiry** | % of total outstanding points expiring annually | 4.2% | 3.2% |
| **Service Cost per Inquiry** | Total support cost per loyalty-related ticket | $8.50 (2.2M inquiries/yr) | $6.50 (40% AI deflection) |

## Tier 3: Business Impact & Revenue

| Metric | Definition | Current | 2026 Q4 Target |
|--------|------------|---------|----------------|
| **Gross Member Revenue** | Total revenue from loyalty members (flights + credit card + partners) | $850M/yr | $1,050M/yr |
| **Member Acquisition Cost** | Total marketing spend per new member | $22/member | $12/member |
| **Contribution Margin** | (Member revenue − Program costs) / Member revenue | 45% | 52% |
| **Portfolio ROI** | Incremental revenue / feature investment across roadmap | 3.2x (historic) | 5.4x (2026 portfolio) |
| **LTV/LLC Ratio** | Member lifetime value / lifetime cost (acquire + service) | 2.3x | 3.1x |

## Dashboard Cadence

| Dashboard | Frequency | Audience |
|-----------|-----------|----------|
| Executive (NPS, Active Members, Redemption Rate, Margin) | Weekly | Leadership |
| Product (app rating, award inventory, funnel, feature performance) | Daily | PM, Engineering |
| Financial (revenue, costs, MAC, margin, ROI) | Monthly | Finance, PM |
| Stakeholder (segment perf, competitive benchmarking) | Quarterly | All stakeholders |

## Measurement Principles

- **Quantitative + qualitative:** System data supplemented by member surveys and interviews.
- **Real-time monitoring** → daily; tactical reviews → weekly; strategic reviews → quarterly.
- **Ownership:** PM (Diana) owns roadmap; Finance owns margin; Operations owns award inventory; Customer Insight owns NPS.
- **Purpose:** Ensure elite tier growth and casual member activation while maintaining ≥50% contribution margin.

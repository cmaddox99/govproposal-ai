# Use Case: Member Acquisition — Growing AADvantage from 3.8M to 5.0M Members

## Overview
This use case walks through how AADvantage uses product laws to acquire 1.2M new members in 2026, focusing on casual travelers and occasional business flyers who don't currently have AADvantage accounts or are inactive.

**Primary Personas:** Sarah (Casual Member), Marcus (Frequent Traveler)  
**Secondary Persona:** Diana (Program Manager)  
**Duration:** 12 months (Q1 2026 — Q4 2026)  
**Success Metric:** 1.2M net new members; 60% activation within 90 days

---

## Phase 1: Discovery & Research (Q1 2026)

### Objectives
- Understand why 2.5M+ eligible frequent flyers *don't* have AADvantage accounts
- Identify barriers to enrollment and early activation
- Validate acquisition opportunity and messaging

### Laws Applied

**PRD-1.1: Continuous Discovery**
- Conduct 300 interviews: non-members, inactive members, competitor program members
- Survey: "Why don't you have AADvantage?" Top reasons expected:
  - Didn't know about program (45%)
  - Enrollment seemed complex (25%)
  - Competitor program perceived as better (20%)
  - Other (10%)
- Competitive analysis: Study United MileagePlus onboarding flow; why is it faster/simpler?
- Behavioral data: Analyze non-members' flight patterns—are they frequent enough to value program?

**PRD-2.1: User Journey Mapping**
- Map non-member journey: Awareness → Consideration → Enrollment → First earning
- Identify drop-off points: "Of 1M website visitors, only 10K complete enrollment"
- Map competitor switcher journey: "Why did Sarah switch from United to AADvantage?"
- Enrollment benchmark: Industry standard <2 min enrollment time; ours is 4.5 min

### Outputs
- Discovery report: 3 key barriers identified (complexity, perception, awareness)
- Journey map: Current vs. best-practice enrollment flow
- Competitive feature comparison: Where we're weak (speed) vs. strong (benefits clarity)
- Persona profiles: Who are the 1.2M we want to acquire? (60% leisure, 40% business occasional)

### Success Metrics (Q1 Baseline)
- Enrollment completion rate: 10% of website visitors → **Target Q2: 15%**
- Enrollment time: 4.5 min → **Target Q2: 2.5 min**
- Post-enrollment activation: 35% within 30 days → **Target Q2: 55%**

---

## Phase 2: Roadmap Planning (Q1-Q2 2026)

### Objectives
- Prioritize acquisition features based on impact/effort
- Create roadmap to remove enrollment barriers
- Plan messaging & marketing campaigns

### Laws Applied

**PRD-3.1: Roadmap Planning**
- Prioritize by persona impact:
  - High impact (Score 80+): Simplify enrollment (SSO login), first-flight bonus, mobile app
  - Medium impact (Score 65-80): Credit card integration, social proof
  - Lower priority (Score <65): Gamification, achievements

**Tier 1 Features (Q1-Q2, $2.5M investment):**
1. One-click enrollment (SSO with Google/Apple ID) → 50% faster
2. Mobile-optimized enrollment page → works on any device
3. First-flight bonus (2,500 points) → immediate gratification
4. Email confirmation with "next steps" → reduces confusion

**Tier 2 Features (Q2-Q3, $1.2M investment):**
1. In-flight signup → acquire on plane before first flight completes
2. Partner pre-enrollment (hotels, car rentals) → acquire through partners
3. Credit card co-enrollment → combine card + loyalty signup

### Outputs
- Feature roadmap: Prioritized by acquisition impact
- Campaign calendar: When/where to promote each channel
- Partner strategy: Which airlines, credit card partners, and travel brands to work with
- Success metrics: Enrollment rate targets by channel

### Success Metrics (Q2 Target)
- Enrollment completion rate: 10% → **15%** (+50%)
- Enrollment time: 4.5 min → **2.0 min** (includes SSO + auto-fill)
- New members from in-flight signup: 0% → **10% of new members**
- Credit card co-enrollment: 0% → **15% of new members**

---

## Phase 3: Implementation & MVP Validation (Q2-Q3 2026)

### Objectives
- Build and test enrollment features
- Validate messaging with small cohorts
- Scale winning tactics

### Laws Applied

**PRD-4.1: MVP & Product-Market Fit**

**MVP 1: Simplified Enrollment (Q2)**
- One-click SSO enrollment (Google, Apple ID, Facebook)
- Auto-fill from Google account: Name, email, date of birth
- 5-field form (reduced from 7): Email, DOB, city, frequent destinations (optional), travel frequency
- Immediate rewards: "You've earned 2,500 bonus points! Use them on your next flight"

**Validation Plan:**
- A/B test: New SSO flow vs. current 7-field form with 10K users each
- Metrics: Conversion rate, completion time, abandonment rate
- Success criteria: >20% improvement in completion rate

**Test Results (Week 4 of MVP):**
- Completion rate: Old form 10% → New SSO 15% ✅ (+50% vs. target +20%)
- Enrollment time: 4.5 min → 1.8 min ✅ (target was 2.0 min)
- User feedback: "Much faster. I actually signed up this time."
- Go/No-go decision: **GO** → Broad launch in Q3

**MVP 2: First-Flight Bonus (Q2)**
- New members receive 2,500 bonus points immediately after enrollment
- Email incentive: "Fly within 7 days and earn 2,500 bonus points"
- Reinforcement: "You now have 2,500 points. Check out redemption options for your next trip"

**Validation Plan:**
- Test messaging: 3 variations of bonus email
- Metrics: Email open rate, enrollment within 7 days, redemption rate
- Success criteria: >40% of new enrollees take first flight within 30 days

**Test Results:**
- Email open rate: 34% (industry avg 20%)
- First flight within 30 days: 48% ✅ (target was 40%)
- Bonus redemption: 22% of first-flight earners use bonus points immediately
- Go/No-go decision: **GO** → Broad launch in Q3

**MVP 3: In-Flight Signup (Q3)**
- Pilot on 50 flights: Offer enrollment during seatback in-flight entertainment
- Form: Email + password (takes 60 seconds)
- Activation: Points credited when enrollment completes (no need to wait)
- Benefit: Member starts earning from that flight immediately

**Validation Plan:**
- Test 2 messaging variations: "Join AADvantage on this flight" vs. "Earn 1,500 bonus points today"
- Track: Signup rate per flight, completion rate, post-flight engagement
- Success criteria: >5% of passengers on selected flights enroll

**Test Results (8 weeks of testing):**
- Overall signup rate: 6.2% of passengers ✅ (target 5%)
- Completion rate: 78% (many drop off at password confirmation)
- Demographics: 65% leisure, 35% business (intended target)
- Go/No-go decision: **GO CONDITIONAL** → Expand to 500 flights in Q3 but simplify password step

### Outputs
- Launch-ready features: SSO enrollment, first-flight bonus, in-flight signup
- Messaging framework: Personalized by channel (email, in-flight, partner)
- Operations plan: How to scale from MVP to 1M+ new members

### Success Metrics (Q3 Target)
- New member acquisitions: 150K (Q1) + 200K (Q2) + 300K (Q3) = 650K YTD
- Enrollment conversion: 15% broad (up from 10% baseline)
- 30-day activation rate: 60% (up from 35%)
- Projected annual new members: 1.0M+ (on pace for 1.2M target)

## Phase 4: Launch, Scale & Measurement (Q3-Q4 2026)

### Objectives
- Scale winning features to all channels
- Acquire 550K remaining members to hit 1.2M target
- Measure incremental impact and ROI

### Laws Applied

**PRD-5.1: Metrics & Success Definition**

**Acquisition Channel Performance (Q3-Q4):**

| Channel | Q1-Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | Full Year | CAC |
|---------|-------|----|----|-----------|-----|
| Website (SSO) | 120K | 180K | 150K | 450K | $8 |
| In-Flight | 0 | 80K | 120K | 200K | $5 |
| Credit Card | 0 | 45K | 60K | 105K | $3 |
| Partner (hotels) | 45K | 60K | 80K | 185K | $6 |
| Referral (gifting) | 0 | 15K | 60K | 75K | $2 |
| **Total** | **165K** | **380K** | **470K** | **1.015M** | **$5.50 avg** |

**Key Metrics Dashboard:**

| Metric | Target | Q3 Actual | Q4 Forecast | Status |
|--------|--------|-----------|-------------|--------|
| New members acquired | 1.2M | 650K (54% of annual) | 1.015M | On track |
| Enrollment conversion rate | 18% | 16% | 15% (seasonal dip) | Slight miss |
| 30-day activation rate | 65% | 62% | 60% (ramp-up effect) | Close |
| Member acquisition cost | $10 | $5.80 | $5.50 | Beating target |
| Total program members | 5.0M | 4.85M | 4.95M | 99% of target |

**Channel Performance Insights:**

1. **In-flight signup:** Best performer after launch (6.2% signup rate)
   - Action: Expand from 50 to 1,000 flights by Q4
   - Opportunity: Add partnership with flight attendants ("Ask your flight attendant for AADvantage help")

2. **Credit card co-enrollment:** Lowest CAC ($3/member)
   - Action: Streamline co-enrollment flow (currently 2 forms; consolidate to 1)
   - Opportunity: Launch exclusive card member welcome bonus

3. **Referral/gifting:** Emerging strong (only started Q3)
   - Action: Once gifting feature launches fully, projectthis channel to 150K+ members in 2027
   - Opportunity: "Refer 5 friends, earn 5,000 points"

4. **Partner channel:** Steady performer
   - Action: Expand from 5 hotel partners to 15 by Q4
   - Opportunity: "Book Marriott, earn AADvantage points + hotel points"

**Activation Funnel (Members → Active Users):**

```
1M new members enrolled
  ↓
620K complete first profile (email verification, preferences) — 62%
  ↓
450K take first flight or earn points — 60% of completers = 45% overall
  ↓
300K actively use program (2+ earning events) — 67% of first-flyers = 30% overall
  ↓
Target: 60% 30-day activation = 600K → **Actual: 62% = 620K** ✅
```

**Post-Launch Optimization (Q4):**

- Identify drop-off: Why do 38% not activate within 30 days?
  - Survey findings: 45% forgot enrollment, 30% travel less frequent than expected, 25% preferred competitor
  - Action: Re-engagement campaign for inactive members; targeted offers

- Optimize messaging by channel:
  - Email (website enrollees): More travel tips; less product features
  - In-flight: Quick activation tips (lounge, upgrades, benefits)
  - Partner: Emphasize earning through partner (hotel, car rental)

- Retention focus: 30-day activation is just beginning
  - Monitor 60-day and 90-day retention
  - Target: 85% of activated members continue engaging

### Success Metrics (Q4 Final)
- Total new members acquired: 1.015M (85% of 1.2M target) — **Reason: Seasonal travel dip in Dec hurt Q4 new signups**
- Total program membership: 4.95M (98% of 5.0M target)
- Member acquisition cost: $5.50 (beat $10 target)
- 30-day activation rate: 62% (beat 60% target)
- Incremental revenue from new members: $45-50M annually (LTV $45-50 per new casual member)

### Lessons Learned

**What Worked Well:**
1. **In-flight signup:** Lowest CAC ($5), highest engagement (6.2% passengers)
   - Scale to 100% of flights in 2027
2. **Credit card co-enrollment:** Highest quality (only lowest-CAC members)
   - Deepen partnership with AmEx
3. **Mobile-first experience:** 70% of enrollments on mobile
   - Ensure all features mobile-optimized
4. **Referral/gifting:** Emerging winner; will grow 4x in 2027
   - Invest heavily in viral mechanics

**What Didn't Work:**
1. **Direct email campaigns:** Low conversion (2% click-through)
   - Shift from push to pull; in-app offers instead
2. **Partner expansion too slow:** Wanted 20 partners; achieved 15
   - Simplify partner onboarding process for 2027

**2027 Planning:**
- Target: 2.0M net new members (vs. 1.2M in 2026)
- Channels: Prioritize in-flight (scale 10x), gifting (emerging), partnerships (expand 2x)
- Investment: $25M (vs. $8M in 2026 to acquire 1.2M)

---

## Use Case Summary

**Problem Solved:** 2.5M+ eligible frequent flyers not using AADvantage; program membership plateauing at 3.8M

**Solution:** Simplify enrollment (SSO), add in-flight signup, integrate with credit card and travel partners

**Results:** 1.015M new members acquired; 5-year blended ROI 3.5x (new member LTV $45-50 × 1M = $45-50M revenue vs. $8M investment)

**Next Steps:** Optimize 30-day+ retention to ensure new members become long-term valuable members; plan 2027 member acquisition growth to 2M+ new members.

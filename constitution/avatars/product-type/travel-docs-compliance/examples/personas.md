# TravelDocs Personas

## Persona 1: International Passenger

- Goal: Know exactly what travel documents are needed before airport arrival.
- Pain points: Ambiguous requirements, changing country rules, late surprises.
- Success signal: Clear readiness status with actionable next steps.

## Persona 2: Check-In Agent

- Goal: Resolve not-ready passengers quickly without manual research.
- Pain points: Incomplete context from upstream systems, unclear reason codes.
- Success signal: One-screen diagnosis and remediation guidance.

## Persona 3: Compliance Analyst

- Goal: Keep policy interpretation aligned with regulatory requirements.
- Pain points: Rule drift across integrations and environments.
- Success signal: Auditable rule changes and explainable outcomes.

## Persona 4: Operations Manager

- Goal: Maintain departure throughput with minimal document-related escalations.
- Pain points: Late readiness failures and provider instability during peaks.
- Success signal: Route-level dashboards and proactive alerts.

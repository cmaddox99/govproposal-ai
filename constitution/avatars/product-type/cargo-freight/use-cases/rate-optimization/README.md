# Cargo Use Case: Rate Optimization

**Overview:**  
Dynamic pricing strategy to maximize cargo revenue by adjusting rates based on demand, capacity utilization, and competitive positioning.

**Personas Involved:**
- Roberto (Partner Airline - capacity manager)
- Diana (Cargo PM - revenue optimization)
- Pricing algorithm + data team

**Value Delivered:**
- 2-5% margin improvement through demand-based pricing
- Real-time capacity visibility for all routes
- Automated rate adjustments reduce manual work

---

## Workflow Summary

### Phase 1: Discovery - Understanding Revenue Dynamics
- Research: How do competitors adjust pricing? What signals drive demand?
- Data: Current prices are static (set quarterly) - reactive not proactive
- Pain: Revenue left on table when flights are 80%+ full

### Phase 2: Planning - Dynamic Pricing Strategy
- Roadmap: Q2-Q3 2026 implement demand-based pricing
- MVP: Pilot on top 10 routes with highest volume
- Success: Margin +2-3% vs. static pricing, zero oversells

### Phase 3: Implementation - Pricing Engine Enhancement
- Data inputs: Current capacity utilization, time-to-departure, historical demand
- Algorithm: Adjust rates up as capacity fills, down as departure nears (reverse)
- Output: Real-time rate updates, sent to all booking channels

### Phase 4: Launch & Measure
- Pilot: 10 high-volume routes (4 weeks)
- Metrics: Margin improvement, fill rate, revenue per flight
- Scale: All routes if pilot successful

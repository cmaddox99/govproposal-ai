# PRD-1.5 Evidence-Based — Passenger Booking

## Law Summary

Investment decisions must be backed by quantified data. Compliance requirements are evidence, not optional context.

---

## ✅ COMPLIANT Example

### Decision

> Invest in displaying all-in price (base fare + taxes + mandatory fees) in search results before checkout.

### Hypothesis

> Showing the total price at search results will reduce payment-step abandonment from 28% to ≤ 22% within 60 days of launch, and will bring the booking flow into compliance with DOT 14 CFR Part 399.

### Evidence Package

| Evidence Point | Value | Source |
|---------------|-------|--------|
| Payment-step abandonment rate (current) | 28% | Funnel analytics (3-month, 4.2M sessions) |
| #1 verbatim cause of payment abandonment | "Total price was higher than expected" | Usability tests (20 sessions) |
| A/B test: conversion lift when total price shown at search | +12% booking completion | A/B test (3-week, 180K sessions per arm) |
| DOT 14 CFR Part 399 compliance status | Non-compliant | Legal/Compliance audit |
| DOT enforcement risk (industry precedent) | $50K–$500K per violation event | DOT enforcement actions database |
| Booking sessions/month losing at payment step | ~1.2M | Calculated: 4.2M × 28% |
| Average booking value lost per abandonment | $380 | Booking value analysis |
| Revenue recovery potential at 6pp abandonment reduction | +$27M/year | Calculated: 1.2M × 6% × $380 |

### A/B Test Evidence

**Test:** 3-week A/B on aa.com search results. Treatment: all-in price displayed. Control: current base-fare display.

| Arm | Sessions | Booking Completion Rate |
|-----|----------|------------------------|
| Control (base fare) | 180,000 | 37.2% |
| Treatment (all-in price) | 180,000 | 41.6% |
| Lift | — | +12% relative (+4.4pp absolute) |

**Statistical significance:** p < 0.001, 95% CI [+3.8pp, +5.0pp].

### Decision

Evidence package is complete. Two independent drivers:
1. **Compliance:** DOT 14 CFR Part 399 mandates all-in price disclosure. Not displaying is a violation regardless of conversion outcome.
2. **Revenue:** +12% booking completion lift confirmed in A/B test; estimated +$27M/year.

Investment proceeds.

---

## ❌ VIOLATION Example

> "Price transparency sounds like a good idea. Customers probably want to see total price. Let's add it to the checkout page with a breakdown of fees."

**Why this violates PRD-1.5:**
- "Sounds like a good idea" is not evidence.
- Showing breakdown at checkout (not at search) does not address the abandonment root cause OR the DOT compliance requirement.
- No A/B test, no funnel data, no compliance citation.
- Correct approach: A/B test showing all-in price at search (not checkout). The compliance requirement makes this non-negotiable regardless of A/B result.

# PRD-6.2 Retention Law — Passenger Booking

## Law Summary

Retention is the north-star metric. Loyal customer experience investment has higher ROI than new customer acquisition. Gate acquisition spend on retention performance.

---

## ✅ COMPLIANT Example

### Context

The Marketing team is proposing a $2.1M Q3 acquisition campaign targeting new-to-AA leisure travelers. The Booking product team challenges the investment allocation.

### Evidence

**Booking completion rate by AAdvantage status (3-month cohort, 4.2M sessions):**

| Segment | Booking Completion Rate | Search-to-Book Rate |
|---------|------------------------|---------------------|
| AAdvantage Executive Platinum | 74% | 18% |
| AAdvantage Platinum Pro | 68% | 14% |
| AAdvantage Gold | 61% | 11% |
| AAdvantage Member (no status) | 38% | 4% |
| Non-member (anonymous session) | 22% | 2% |

**90-Day Rebook Rate by AAdvantage Status:**

| Segment | 90-Day Rebook Rate | Average Revenue/Customer/Year |
|---------|--------------------|-------------------------------|
| Executive Platinum | 91% | $14,200 |
| Platinum Pro | 84% | $8,600 |
| Gold | 71% | $4,100 |
| AAdvantage Member | 43% | $1,200 |
| Non-member (converted) | 28% | $620 |

**Finding:** An AAdvantage status member (any tier) has 3× the booking completion rate of a non-member session. A new customer acquired through the Q3 campaign has a 28% 90-day rebook rate and $620 average annual revenue, vs. $4,100+ for a Gold member.

### Investment Comparison

| Investment | Cost | Projected 12-Month Revenue Impact |
|------------|------|----------------------------------|
| Q3 acquisition campaign (new-to-AA) | $2.1M | ~$3.2M (based on projected new customers × $620 avg value) |
| All-in price transparency (Gold member retention) | $280K | ~$4.8M (Gold member churn reduction × $4,100 avg value) |
| AAdvantage status member booking flow prioritization | $150K | ~$2.1M (conversion rate lift in status segments) |

### Retention Metric (North Star)

**AAdvantage active member 90-day rebook rate** — tracked monthly, segmented by tier. "Active member" = at least one booking in the last 12 months.

### Investment Gate

Q3 acquisition campaign proceeds only if: AAdvantage active member 90-day rebook rate is ≥ 80% (current: 74%). If retention is below threshold, the acquisition budget is redirected to retention improvement — price transparency, booking flow friction reduction for status members, mobile experience.

---

## ❌ VIOLATION Example

> "New customer acquisition grows the AAdvantage member base. More members = more revenue. Approve Q3 campaign."

**Why this violates PRD-6.2:**
- New-to-AA member LTV ($620) is 6.6× lower than Gold member LTV ($4,100).
- Acquiring more low-retention members while high-value members have booking friction is a value-destroying sequence.
- 90-day rebook rate for new acquisitions (28%) means 72% of the $2.1M acquisition investment walks out in 90 days.
- Correct decision: retention gate on status member 90-day rebook rate before acquisition spend increases.

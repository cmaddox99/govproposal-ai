# PRD-1.5: Evidence-Based Decisions

All feature prioritisation must cite quantitative evidence. Gut-feel proposals are rejected at discovery gate.

## Application to Customer Relations Ops

This product domain applies PRD-1.5 by requiring all feature work to demonstrate compliance
with the evidence-based decisions principle before advancing through the stage gate.

See [PRD Article I](../../../../laws/product/foundations.md) and [PRD Article II](../../../../laws/product/discovery.md) for full law text.

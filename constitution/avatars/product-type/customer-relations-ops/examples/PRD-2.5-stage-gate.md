# PRD-2.5: Stage-Gate Review

Each product increment passes through: Problem Gate → Solution Gate → Build Gate → Launch Gate. No gate may be skipped.

## Application to Customer Relations Ops

This product domain applies PRD-2.5 by requiring all feature work to demonstrate compliance
with the stage-gate review principle before advancing through the stage gate.

See [PRD Article I](../../../../laws/product/foundations.md) and [PRD Article II](../../../../laws/product/discovery.md) for full law text.

## Tier 2 Features — Q2-Q3 2026 (Score 65-80)

### 2.1 Predictive Oversell Management (Score: 78)
**Problem:** Oversell discovered at gate — volunteer scramble (20 min), angry passengers, boarding delays.  
**Solution:** Predictive model 30 min pre-departure; automated SMS to standbys; rebooking automation for confirmed volunteers.  
**Business value:** Boarding delay reduced 20 min → 2 min per oversell incident; passenger satisfaction on oversold flights 5→8/10.  
**Effort:** 75 days

### 2.2 Baggage Tracking Integration (Score: 72)
**Problem:** Maria doesn't know if her bag made it. Separate system — no visibility in check-in app.  
**Solution:** Real-time baggage tracking in app; SMS notification when bag loaded on aircraft.  
**Business value:** Reduced baggage anxiety calls; loyalty trust improvement.  
**Effort:** 60 days

### 2.3 Biometric Boarding Pilot (Score: 68)
**Problem:** Mobile still requires barcode scan. Alex's ideal: walk through gate without stopping.  
**Solution:** Facial recognition boarding pilot on 5 gates at ATL. Opt-in pre-registration.  
**Business value:** <5 sec per passenger; innovation leadership; foundation for full rollout.  
**Note:** Requires regulatory approval, biometric privacy impact assessment (BUS-4.1), hardware procurement.  
**Effort:** 120 days

## Scoring Methodology

**Passenger Impact (40 pts max):**
- Convenience improvement (0-10)
- Reliability improvement (0-10)  
- Satisfaction delta (0-10)
- Accessibility improvement (0-10)

**Operational Impact (40 pts max):**
- Boarding time reduction (0-10)
- Labour efficiency (0-10)
- System reliability gain (0-10)
- Cost savings (0-10)

**Effort (20 pts max, inverse):**
- Development days: ≤30d=20, ≤90d=16, ≤120d=17, >180d=0
- Architecture risk: low=high score, high=low score
- Integration dependencies: none=20, many=0

### Persona 1: "Alex" — Digital Traveler

**Templated Profile:**
- Role: Corporate executive, frequent business traveler
- Age: 38 | Experience: 15+ flights/year | Device: iPhone + laptop
- Behavior: Books 3 weeks ahead, checks in online 24h before

**Templated Goals:**
1. Zero friction — check in, board, deplane without talking to agents
2. Mobile-first — never interact with kiosk or counter
3. Speed — airport arrival → gate in 15-30 minutes

**Templated Pain Points:**
1. Mobile check-in fails at gate; forced to counter for duplicate boarding pass
2. App logs out between sessions; must re-authenticate
3. WiFi unavailable; can't check bags digitally
4. Boarding announcements unclear

**Templated Quote:**
> "If I have to talk to an agent, the process has failed."

### Persona 3: "Kevin" — Gate Agent

**Templated Profile:**
- Role: Ground operations, gate service representative
- Age: 45 | Experience: 12 years | Manages: 200+ passengers/flight, 6-8 flights daily
- Tools: Biometric scanner, gate display system, radio

**Templated Goals:**
1. Get flight out on time (80% of day's success depends on this)
2. Minimize boarding exceptions (oversold, standbys, accessible passengers)
3. Keep passengers informed and moving smoothly
4. Handle problems (tech failures, passenger confusion) quickly

**Templated Pain Points:**
1. Check-in backlog — some passengers aren't in system at gate
2. Overselling — too many passengers; angry standbys
3. Scanner failures — biometric doesn't read; manual lookup
4. Baggage issues — gate-checked bags not updated in real time
5. Accessibility — needs clear process for wheelchair, visual, deaf
6. Communication gaps — doesn't know who's on flight until gate opens

**Templated Quote:**
> "If the system is working, I can board 600 passengers in 35 minutes. If it's broken, we're looking at 60 minutes and angry customers."

### Persona 4: "Patricia" — Operations Manager

**Templated Profile:**
- Role: Senior airport operations manager
- Age: 52 | Experience: 20 years | Oversees: 900+ departures daily
- Manages: 8 gates, 30 agents, 180K+ passengers daily

**Templated Goals:**
1. On-time departures >82%
2. Operational efficiency <38 min average boarding
3. Passenger satisfaction >80%
4. System uptime >99.5%
5. Accessibility compliance

**Templated Pain Points:**
1. Peak hours (6-8am, 5-7pm) bottlenecks
2. Technology debt — check-in system built in 2015
3. Staffing — manual processes need 15-20 agents/gate
4. Overselling — automation could predict better
5. Integration gaps — baggage, loyalty, special services not connected
6. Cascade failures — check-in outage → gate backlog → missed on-time

**Templated Quote:**
> "If I can reduce boarding time from 40 to 35 minutes, we hit 85% on-time and save $2M annually."
